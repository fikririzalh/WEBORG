HOW TO PLAY

Outsmart the hunter.

01Wolf moves in secret

One orthogonal tile every round. Its position from two rounds ago is revealed to the Bunnies.

02Bunnies coordinate

Three Bunnies plan one orthogonal move each. They can see one another and share one key.

03Find the key

Stop on the hidden key tile. Nearby Bunnies receive distance clues before it is found.

04Open an exit

The key holder spends a turn opening a gate. If not caught, the gate stays open permanently.

05Survive the Wolf

Same destination means capture. Swapping tiles does not. Round one is safe.

06Beat the clock

All Bunnies must escape by round 12. Three captures end the hunt for the Wolf.
