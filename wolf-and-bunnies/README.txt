WOLF & BUNNIES - Hidden Hunt prototype

Run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. No build step is required.

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- app.js

Prototype rules implemented:
- 5x5 board
- 1 Wolf vs 3 Bunnies
- Orthogonal movement only
- Private Wolf setup and hidden movement via pass-device overlay
- One hidden shared key with placement constraints
- Two identical exit gates
- Rolling Wolf reveal with a two-round delay
- Immediate Wolf reveal after a capture
- Three shared Bunny lives
- Captured Bunny skips one round, then respawns
- Round 1 safe from capture
- 12-round match limit
- Bunny key proximity hints
