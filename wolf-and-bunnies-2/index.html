HOW TO PLAY

Outsmart the hunter.

016×6 hidden movement

Wolf and Bunnies move one orthogonal tile. In Normal Hunt, contact is lethal to the Bunny: if Wolf enters a Bunny tile, or a Bunny enters Wolf's current tile, that Bunny is caught. Wolf movement is private and its trail is revealed with a two-round delay.

02Find and escort the key

Three Bunnies coordinate to locate the hidden shared key and escort its carrier toward either exit.

03Wolf steals the key

If Wolf catches the Bunny carrying the key, Wolf takes it and its current position is immediately exposed.

04Counter Hunt

For the next two rounds the roles reverse. If Wolf enters a Bunny tile or a Bunny enters Wolf's tile, the contact hits Wolf and recovers the key instead of killing the Bunny.

05Wolf survives the chase

If Bunnies fail to hit Wolf during both Counter Hunt rounds, Wolf throws the key to a valid random tile and normal hunting resumes.

06One Puff each

Wolf and every Bunny have one random relocation per match. A character carrying the key cannot use Puff.

07Open an exit

The Bunny key holder spends one turn opening a gate. An opened gate remains open for the rest of the match.

08Win conditions

All Bunnies must escape by round 14. Wolf wins if the shared three lives are depleted or time expires.
