Source visual material:
Anomia Print & Play — Create Your Own Deck!, Anomia Press.

Modifications in this remix include:
- extraction/cropping of card-back and symbol artwork from the supplied Print & Play PDF;
- an original cat-themed web interface;
- an original 150-category editable card library;
- browser-based CRUD, local storage, game flow, scoring, Wild Card, and cascade logic.
